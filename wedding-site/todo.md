**Details:**

## 2. Add to Calendar Button
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐ Easy  
**Details:** 
- Add buttons for "Add to Google Calendar" and "Add to Apple Calendar".
- **Google Calendar:** We generate a simple link format (`https://calendar.google.com/calendar/render?...`) containing the wedding date, time, and location.
- **Apple/Outlook:** We generate a downloadable `.ics` file link.
- Can be added directly to the "Celebrate With Us" cards or right below the Hero section.

## 3. RSVP Section
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐ Medium (Only because it requires choosing a data destination)  
**Details:** 
- Create a beautiful form at the bottom of the website containing fields for Name, Attending (Yes/No), Meal Preferences, and Plus Ones.
- Hook the "Submit" button up to a free service like Web3Forms (to email you the RSVPs directly) or a Google Apps Script (to instantly save RSVPs into a Google Sheet).
